1. Why are the variables declared as private?

The variables are declared as private to prevent them from being accessed or changed directly from outside the class.

2. What happens if the variables are declared public?

If the variables are public, other classes can directly access and modify the values.

3. Why do we use getters and setters?

Getters are used to retrieve the values of private variables, while setters are used to modify the values in a controlled way.
